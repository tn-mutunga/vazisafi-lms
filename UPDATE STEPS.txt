and choose Replace (desktop and supabase folders here are complete, so replacing is safe).

NEW IN 2.6.1 — QR SIGN-UP (for the USIU pageant table)
- Customers scan a QR code, fill in name + phone on their phone, and are added to Customers
  by themselves (tagged "USIU Pageant"), with 15% off their FIRST order, valid 14 days.
- People who are already customers can scan too: they are thanked, but get no discount.
- New Order: pick the customer (search name or phone) → "WELCOME OFFER" banner, 15% already
  in the Discount box. Nothing to type. After 14 days the banner says the offer has expired.
- Customers → "QR sign-ups" chip: the list of sign-ups plus live numbers (sign-ups, today,
  first orders, sales from them, discount given, existing customers who scanned).
  It syncs by itself every minute while open; "Sync now" for an instant refresh.
- Management → Settings → QR sign-up: campaign on/off, %, days, QR code, poster download.
- (from 2.6.0) Job cards: 10 prints per order. Customers → Joined shows the time.

ONE-TIME SET-UP (about 15 minutes, do it TONIGHT)
1. Supabase → SQL Editor → paste the CONTENTS of supabase/11_qr_signup.sql → Run.
2. Publish 2.6.1 and install it on your Mac (Check for updates).
3. Management → Settings → QR sign-up → "Download sign-up page" (saves index.html).
4. Put it online: make a new folder called vazisafi-join with only that index.html in it.
   Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop (free sign-up with Google or email) and drag the folder in.
   Then Site configuration → Change site name → vazisafi-join
   Your address becomes https://vazisafi-join.netlify.app (or whatever name was free).
5. Back in Settings → QR sign-up: paste that address in "Sign-up page address".
   New campaign: type USIU Pageant → Add. Check 15% and 14 days.
   Then tap the sync pill at the top so the cloud knows about the campaign.
6. Test with your phone: scan the QR on the screen, sign up with a number that is NOT already
   a customer. Within a minute it shows in Customers → QR sign-ups. Delete the test customer after.
7. "Download QR poster" → open the file → print on A4. Print 2–3 copies for the table.

AT THE EVENT
- Keep a laptop (with internet) open on Customers → QR sign-ups: the count rises as people sign up.
- After the event, switch the campaign off if you like: the page then says "This offer has ended".
  People who signed up keep their 14 days either way.
