VAZI SAFI LMS v2.2.3
- FIX: a laptop could fall permanently behind the cloud. If a sync was cut short by
  "Pull cloud", it still saved its "already fetched up to here" marker, so those
  orders were never fetched again. Now the marker is only saved when the sync finishes.
- NEW safety check on every sync: the laptop compares its order list with the cloud's
  and downloads any order it is missing. Cloud & Updates shows
  "Orders: cloud / this laptop" with a tick when they match.
- Revenue Today card: Laundry (sky blue) and Dry Cleaning (purple) on line 1,
  Shoes (orange) and Other (grey) on line 2. All four show even at KES 0.
