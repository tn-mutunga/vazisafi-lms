ALL SCREENS
- Zoom in the app menu: VaziSafi LMS → Zoom in / Zoom out / Actual size (100%).
  (Mac: the VaziSafi LMS menu next to the Apple. Windows: the VaziSafi LMS menu at the top.)
  Shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd + (bigger), Ctrl/Cmd − (smaller), Ctrl/Cmd 0 (100%). 70% to 150%.
- Each laptop remembers its own zoom (not shared between laptops).
- Receipts and job cards still print at 100% whatever the screen zoom.
