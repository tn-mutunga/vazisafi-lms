VAZI SAFI LMS v2.2.9
- New Order tile reads "Wash & Fold (per piece)"; receipts print "Wash & Fold (per pc)".
- Receipt and preview show the customer's first name only.
- Front Desk dashboard: Overdue / Uncollected card under Live machines (oldest first, red -> yellow),
  month-end reminder and a printable uncollected list.
- Reports -> Custom range: pick any From / To dates (2016 onwards) plus quick ranges.
- Printing: blank PDFs fixed (receipt, job card, delivery note, reports).
- Job card & delivery note: 80mm roll or A4 (per laptop). Roll is compact; delivery note starts a new page so the printer auto-cuts.
- Print limits: receipt and delivery note once per order; job card 5 times. Management -> order -> Print permissions -> "Allow 1 more".
- Expenses: "Logged by" (Management on any laptop not signed in with the shop login); deleting needs the PIN.
- Data & Backup: monthly export of orders (with client details, day and month totals) and customers, CSV or JSON.
