VAZI SAFI LMS v2.2.7 (includes everything in 2.2.6)
EXPENSES
- Newest first. "Paid by" is now "Recorded by"; anything logged from Management shows Management.
- Log expense: Cash removed (M-Pesa / Bank). Transaction code min. 5 characters (ideally last 5).
- Outsourced dry cleaning must link at least one order. Linked orders / customers take several;
  typing an order number autocompletes and fills its customer.
ORDERS
- Teller column on Orders; picking a date shows who was on the till that day.
- Dry-cleaning-only orders: Intake -> Dry cleaning -> Ready -> Collected.
- New tiles: Wash & Fold (per piece, teller enters price) and Laundry Express (50% of laundry value,
  dry cleaning and shoes excluded). The % is set in Settings -> Laundry Express Service;
  Express always prints as the last line.
SMS
- Settings -> Stage SMS: switch each stage on/off (synced). On = always sent, Off = never sent.
RECEIPT
- Space after Date and Cashier; Client # and Cashier aligned right.
CUSTOMERS
- Sort box: Most/Fewest orders, Highest/Lowest spend, Most/Least loyal (plus name and joined).
MORE
- Ironing-only orders: Intake -> Ironing -> Ready -> Collected. Every stage has a default SMS (incl. dry cleaning).
- Orders subtitle shows students / normal / corporate counts; Teller column removed (tellers listed in subtitle).
- Record payment: order search with autocomplete; transaction code min. 5 everywhere; -EG hidden.
v2.2.7
- Wash & Fold (per piece): prices set by Management (default KES 200), no teller entry.
- Trench coats, jackets & shukas merged into Wash & Fold (per piece) with their prices; old orders moved over.
- Phone number required for new customers, and for any customer before an order is saved.
