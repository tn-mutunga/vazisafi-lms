NEW IN 2.6.4
- FIX: paid orders showing as due again. Each laptop sends the whole order to the cloud; a
  laptop that had not yet received a payment could send "paid 0" over it (most often in the
  big re-sync after an update). Now an order's Paid can never be less than the payment lines
  recorded for it: fixed on start-up, after every download, and before every upload, so
  wrongly-due orders correct themselves on every laptop and in the cloud.
  Refunds now add a minus payment line ("REFUND") so the books still agree.
- Dashboards (Front Desk and Management): "Balances due · KES …" button opens a pop-up of
  everyone who owes money: total owed, number of customers and unpaid orders, search, sort
  (biggest balance / owing longest / name), and how long each has owed (red after 14 days).
  Tap a customer to see their unpaid orders; tap an order to open it and collect.
- Payments & Audit (Management): "Not recorded · N" button opens a pop-up of money that is on the
  uploaded M-Pesa/bank statements but whose code is on no payment in the system: code, date,
  amount and the payer details from the statement (e.g. "Customer Transfer from 2547… - NAME").
  It suggests the likely order (payer's phone or name matches a customer who owes, or the same
  amount is owed). "Record to order" records it there with that code, already verified from the
  statement; if it is more than that order owes, the rest goes to the same customer's other
  unpaid orders, oldest first. "Not a customer payment" hides transfers, float top-ups etc.
  Statements uploaded before 2.6.4 show no payer details; upload them again to get them.
- Karibu15 (and any campaign with "Laundry items only" ticked in Settings → QR sign-up): the % comes
  off the LAUNDRY items only (wash & fold, per piece, ironing, duvets/bedding, curtains), never dry
  cleaning, shoes or Express. One time only: once an order with laundry on it has taken the
  discount, it is used up. A dry-cleaning-only or shoes-only order does not use it up.
- Dashboard Overdue / Uncollected: "Show more" now lists up to 100 orders (was 50).
- Running bill: "Download statement (PDF)" button under Pay towards account. Saves a PDF straight to
  Downloads (the folder opens). The file is named after the customer's first name and order number,
  e.g. Timothy-2678. It shows the running bill, paid so far, running balance, the unpaid orders,
  and every order and payment with the balance after each (plus your pay-to details if owing).
- Discount codes: the old KARIBU15 code (15% off the whole order, any customer, any number of
  times) is removed from every laptop. Karibu15 is now only the QR sign-up offer.
- Receipt: points lines (points on payment / points balance) removed. "Points used" stays,
  because it is money off the order.

NEW IN 2.6.2
- Order page → "Running bill" (under Payment), for customers with 2 or more orders:
    · Running bill (all their orders), Paid so far, and Running balance (what is still owed),
      plus each unpaid order with paid / total (tap one to open it).
    · "View statement" button: every order (+) and every payment (−), newest first, with the
      balance left after each, so you see what each payment reduced and what remains.
    · "Pay towards account": one M-Pesa/bank payment spread over the unpaid orders,
      oldest first. It shows which orders get cleared and what is left before you save.
      Each order gets its own payment line with the same code; the integrity scan knows
      this is one split payment, not a reused code.
- Customers: the "USIU Pageant" tag next to a name disappears as soon as they have used the
  offer (their first order), or after 10 Dec 2026 if they never came in. The "QR sign-ups" chip and its numbers stay, so you can still see who
  came from the pageant and what they spent.
- Settings → QR sign-up → "Download sign-up page" now gives the same page as the live one:
  header "Vazi Safi Laundry · Wash · Dry · Fold", "Other" country code, phone number spaced
  as it is typed with no leading 0 (727 550 161), "at the counter or during pick-up", dates like 10th Dec 2026.
