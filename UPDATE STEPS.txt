- Everything from 2.2.9 (print fixes, 80mm job card, print limits, overdue card, custom report range, monthly export, expense rules).
- Receipt: client name bold, terms justified.
- Job card: sign-off boxes spread evenly in 3 columns; no blank page before the delivery note.
